The founder story is self-reported personal experience. Product tests show whether software functions as specified. Any future health or behavior outcome study needs a defined protocol, consent, a comparison where appropriate, real observations, and an honest account of limits. A working planner, a founder transformation, and a customer review do not establish that the product causes weight loss or visible abs.
